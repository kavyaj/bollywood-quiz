Why skip the Bollywood quiz PPT?

A PowerPoint quiz sounds quick until you start making one.

  • An evening of slides

    Finding questions, making answer slides, fixing fonts that break on the office laptop.

  • One fixed order

    A PPT goes slide after slide. On a board, teams pick the category and the points they want to play for.

  • Text only

    A PPT can't easily do song clips, posters that reveal themselves, or a hidden photo.

Good to know

Can I use BollyQuiz for an office or college quiz?

Yes. It's made for groups. Put it on the projector or meeting-room screen, split the room into teams, and let them buzz in.

Does it work on a projector?

Yes. Connect your laptop with an HDMI cable, open bollyquiz.com, and go full screen. The board is designed to be read from across a room.

Can I run it on Zoom or Teams?

Yes. Share the browser tab and turn on "share sound" so everyone hears the song clips.

Can I download it as a PPT?

No. BollyQuiz runs in your browser, so there's nothing to download, edit or fix before the quiz. Open the link and play.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no app and no payment.
