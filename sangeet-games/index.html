Games for delays and waiting time

Sangeets rarely run on schedule. The DJ is still setting up, the couple's entry is late, or dinner is delayed, and a hundred guests are left waiting. Your set comes with a backup of activities and games for exactly those gaps.

  1. 🙋One person runs it. Ask a cousin, a friend or your emcee to take charge, so nobody on the family's main team has to step away.
  2. 🖥️Pull it up on the screen when there's a wait. It runs in a browser, so it opens on the same screen you're already using.
  3. 🪑Guests join from their seats while the sound is sorted, the couple get ready or the food is on its way.

20 sangeet couple game questions (free)

Not ready for a custom board? These sangeet game questions work on their own. Ask the bride and groom separately, then together, and give a point for every answer that matches. Or let the guests guess.

How they met

  1. Where did they first meet?
  2. Who made the first move?
  3. What was their first date?
  4. Who said “I love you” first?
  5. What was the first gift one gave the other?

Who's more likely to

  1. Cry at the vidaai?
  2. Be late to their own wedding?
  3. Win an argument?
  4. Start dancing first tonight?
  5. Forget their anniversary?

The families

  1. Whose mum calls more often?
  2. Who was the first relative they met from the other side?
  3. Who's the favourite with the other's parents?
  4. Whose family has the bigger WhatsApp group?
  5. Which family member will dance the most tonight?

Filmy couple

  1. Which song is “their” song?
  2. Which Bollywood jodi are they most like?
  3. What did they watch on their first movie date?
  4. Who cries more at films?
  5. If their love story were a film, what would it be called?

Sangeet games ideas that work on the night

A sangeet has a lot going on: a hundred-odd guests, three generations, a DJ who wants the floor back, and a schedule that's already running late. The fun games for a sangeet ceremony are the ones that fit around all that.

So keep each game short, make it playable from a chair, and put the couple in the spotlight. Couple games for sangeet night do all three, which is why a quiz about the bride and groom works so well. Everyone in the room knows something the others don't.

Whether you need games for ladies sangeet, games for a sangeet function at home or a big sangeet party, the format stays the same. Only the questions change.

Indian wedding games for the mehndi, haldi and reception

The sangeet gets the dance floor, but the other functions need entertainment too. A couple quiz board works as one of the Indian wedding games for guests at a mehndi or haldi, while hands are drying and nobody can hold a phone.

At the reception, it becomes one of the Indian wedding games for bride and groom on stage: an Indian wedding quiz the whole hall can play along with. Indian wedding couple games and desi wedding games don't need props. They need good stories, and every family has those.

What are good sangeet games for couples?

Sangeet couple games work best when they're about the couple themselves: a couple quiz, "who's more likely to" questions, or a round where the bride and groom answer about each other.

What are good sangeet games?

A couple quiz works for every age: guests answer questions about the bride and groom, or the couple answer about each other. For family and the audience, Bollywood dumb charades and a Bollywood quiz round work well too.
