How to play this quiz

Plug a laptop into the TV, press F11 and pick someone bossy to host. Here's one turn, start to finish.

What you need

  • 📺
    A big screenA laptop on a TV is ideal. It's made to be read from the sofa, so it doesn't work well on phones.
  • 🎤
    One hostHolds the keyboard, reads the questions, settles the arguments. Can play along but can't win. There's an answer sheet link on the board to open on your phone.
  • 🔔
    A buzzerAny free buzzer app that shows who buzzed first. We used MultiBuzz.
  • ✏️
    Paper and a penThe quiz shows questions and answers but doesn't keep score, so someone honest has to.

A turn

  1. Pick a squareThe number on each square is how many points it's worth. 10 is the easiest, 50 the hardest. Whoever got the last one right picks next. For the first pick, the host chooses someone.
  2. The host opens itEveryone sees the question at the same time.
  3. Buzz to answerThe fastest buzzer gets the first guess. Wrong? It passes to the next fastest. Everyone gets one guess per question.
  4. Score itA right answer wins the square's points. If nobody gets it, nobody scores.
  5. The tile liftsThe host shows the answer, goes back to the board, and a bit more of the hidden photo shows through.

Scoring

Right answerThe square's points, from 10 to 50
Wrong answerNo penalty. It passes to the next buzzer
Nobody gets itNo one scores. The tile still lifts
Photo guessed right+100
Photo guessed wrong−50, and no more photo guesses on that board

Each board has 750 points on its squares. Near misses ("Om Shanti" for Om Shanti Om) are the host's call, so agree how strict to be before you start.

The hidden photo

There's a photo under every board. Buzz in and guess it any time.

Right: +100. Wrong: −50, and no more photo guesses on that board.

Why I made this

We were planning a game session and I wanted something more fun than a phone quiz. So I made a board like KVizzing by Kumar Varun, with the questions best suited to our group of friends.

In September 2026, I hosted the first one at home. The board went up on our TV, filmy posters went up on every wall and window.
