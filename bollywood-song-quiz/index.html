About this Bollywood song quiz

You probably know every one of these songs. Naming them without hearing the tune is the hard part.

So every card describes a song three ways: the scene it plays in, the film and year, or the title translated into English. No lyrics are printed here. The first level is songs you've heard at a hundred parties. The second goes back to the 50s, 60s and 70s, where your parents have the advantage.

Every card has a hint and the answer, so it works as a Bollywood song quiz with answers you can play on your own, or read out to a room.

Want more? Try the Bollywood emoji quiz, the dialogues quiz or the 50 Bollywood quiz questions, which has a round of songs too.

Good to know

How does this Bollywood song quiz work?

Each card describes a song without printing its lyrics: a scene, a film and year, or the title translated into English. Guess the song, tap Hint if you are stuck, then tap Show answer to check.

Are the answers included?

Yes. All 20 songs have the song name, the film and year, and a line about who sang or danced to it, hidden behind a tap so you don't see them by accident.

Do I need to understand Hindi?

It helps, but not much. The title cards give the English meaning, and the scene cards describe what happens on screen, so you can still guess.

Can we play it as a group?

Yes. Put this page on a TV and read the cards aloud. For a full game night with real song clips, play First Day First Show, which has a whole category of song clips.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no app and no payment.
