Diwali games for office

Games for Diwali in the office have one job: get people from different teams talking to each other. Both of these are team games, so mix the departments when you split the room.

The setup is a laptop plugged into the meeting-room screen or a projector. That's it. If you were about to build slides for a quiz, you can skip the Bollywood quiz PPT.

Most Diwali office party ideas need someone to shop, print or decorate. These need a host and about an hour. Play one board of the quiz, one of charades, or both if the sweets hold out.

They're not only for Diwali, either. If your team does Fun Friday games at the office, one board fits the slot.

Online Diwali games for remote teams

The quiz works on Zoom or Teams. The host opens the board, shares their screen with sound on for the song round, and teams answer in the chat.

Charades works too. The actor opens the movie picker on their own phone, so only they see the title, and acts it out on camera.

Diwali games for family

At home you've got grandparents, cousins and kids in one room. Mix the ages in every team. The older lot know the black-and-white classics, and the younger lot know last year's releases.

Housefull has a whole category of films from before 1990, and another of one-word titles that kids can act out.

Good to know

What are good Diwali games for the office?

A Bollywood quiz on the big screen and dumb charades both work well. They need no prep, they're played in teams, and they run on any meeting-room screen or projector.

Which Diwali games work for a big group?

Team games scale best. Split a big group into four teams and rotate who buzzes in for the quiz or who acts for charades, so nobody waits long for a turn.

Can we play Diwali games on Zoom or Teams?

Yes. For the quiz, the host opens the board and shares their screen with sound on, and teams answer in the chat. For charades, the actor opens the movie picker on their own phone so only they see the title.
