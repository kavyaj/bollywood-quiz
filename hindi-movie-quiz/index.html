Warm-up: 5 tough ones

These lean old-school. Tap an answer. No timer, nobody's watching.

  1. Question 1 of 5

    What was Amitabh Bachchan's first film?

    Saat Hindustani (1969), about seven Indians who join the fight to free Goa.

  2. Question 2 of 5

    Kishore Kumar was born with which first name?

    Abhas Kumar Ganguly. Ashok and Anoop were his brothers, both actors too.

  3. Question 3 of 5

    Which film was Madhuri Dixit's debut?

    Abodh, in 1984. Tezaab made her a star four years later.

  4. Question 4 of 5

    Who directed Pakeezah (1972)?

    Kamal Amrohi, who was married to its star, Meena Kumari. It took about 14 years to finish.

  5. Question 5 of 5

    Which actor was born Muhammad Yusuf Khan?

    Dilip Kumar, who took his screen name for his first film in 1944.

  1. Question 1 of 5

    Who was the first Indian to win an Oscar?

    Bhanu Athaiya, for the costumes in Gandhi, in 1983. Satyajit Ray's honorary Oscar came in 1992.

  2. Question 2 of 5

    Who won the very first Filmfare Award for Best Actress?

    Meena Kumari, for Baiju Bawra, at the first Filmfare Awards in 1954.

  3. Question 3 of 5

    Which of these did Satyajit Ray make in Hindi?

    Shatranj Ke Khilari (1977), with Sanjeev Kumar and Saeed Jaffrey. The others are in Bengali.

  4. Question 4 of 5

    Who composed the music for Naya Daur (1957)?

    O.P. Nayyar, often called the rhythm king of Hindi film music.

  5. Question 5 of 5

    Who played both twins in Seeta Aur Geeta (1972)?

    Hema Malini. It won her the Filmfare Award for Best Actress.

  1. Question 1 of 5

    Which Bimal Roy film follows a farmer who pulls a rickshaw in Calcutta to save his land?

    Do Bigha Zamin (1953), with Balraj Sahni. It won the first Filmfare Award for Best Film.

  2. Question 2 of 5

    Which film was Shabana Azmi's debut?

    Ankur (1974), directed by Shyam Benegal. It won her the first of five National Awards for Best Actress.

  3. Question 3 of 5

    Who directed and starred in Purab Aur Paschim (1970)?

    Manoj Kumar, whose patriotic roles earned him the nickname Bharat Kumar.

  4. Question 4 of 5

    In which Bimal Roy film does Nutan play a woman in prison for murder?

    Bandini (1963). It was Gulzar's first film as a lyricist.

  5. Question 5 of 5

    Who directed Tumbbad (2018)?

    Rahi Anil Barve, in his debut as a director.

Hindi cinema or Indian cinema?

People search for an Indian cinema quiz, an Indian movie quiz, a Bollywood movie quiz and a Hindi movie quiz, and mostly mean the same thing. They aren't the same thing, though.

Indian cinema is a lot bigger than Bollywood. Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali and Marathi films all have their own stars, classics and trivia. This quiz covers Hindi films only, for now. I'd rather say that up front than have you find out three squares in.

What you do get is Hindi film trivia from old classics to recent releases, in formats you won't find in a list of questions: posters with almost nothing on them, plots told as office jargon, and dialogues read with no feeling at all. For a round of famous lines, try the Bollywood dialogues quiz.

Good to know

Is this Hindi movie quiz free?

Yes. The warm-up and the full quiz board are free, with no sign-up, no app and no payment.

Can I play the Hindi movie quiz on my phone?

The 5-question warm-up works on a phone. The full board is made for a TV or projector, with one host and everyone else answering.

How many questions are there?

The warm-up has 15 questions, played 5 at a time. The board, First Day First Show, has 75 questions across three boards.

Is it an Indian cinema quiz or only Bollywood?

For now it's Hindi cinema only. Films in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and other languages aren't covered yet.

Do I need to speak Hindi to play?

It helps, but it's not essential. Emoji plots, posters and photo rounds are guessable for anyone who's watched a few Hindi films.
