About this famous Bollywood dialogues quiz

Most of us picked up these lines without trying. You probably know more of them than you think.

I picked 30 that are easy to say out loud and hard to forget. The first level is lines you've heard a hundred times. The last one goes back to the 60s and 70s, where your parents have the advantage.

Every card has the line, what it means in English, a hint and the answer. So it works as a Bollywood dialogues quiz with answers you can play on your own, or read out to a room.

Round 2 of the 50 Bollywood quiz questions is all dialogues too. Or try the Bollywood emoji quiz.

Good to know

How does this famous Bollywood dialogues quiz work?

Each card has a famous line from a Hindi film, with its meaning in English. Guess the film, tap Hint for who says it and when, then tap Show answer to check.

Are the answers included?

Yes. All 30 dialogues have the film, the year and a line about the scene, hidden behind a tap so you don't see them by accident.

Do I need to understand Hindi?

It helps, but every Hindi line has its meaning in English underneath, so you can still guess from the scene.

Can we play it as a group?

Yes. Put this page on a TV and read the lines aloud, ideally in your best Raaj Kumar voice. For a full game night, play First Day First Show, which has a whole round of dialogues read completely deadpan.

Yes. No sign-up, no app and no payment.
