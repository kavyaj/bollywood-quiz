What stays off a branded board

Film stills and song clips are owned by studios and music labels, so they are excluded from commercial work.

Every element of your board is written or drawn for you: emoji plots, jargon rounds, trivia, scrambled names, illustrated posters and your own team photographs. Film titles and facts about films may be used.

Good to know

Can we use the free boards for a company event?

Yes. Playing BollyQuiz at work is free. A custom board is only needed if you would like one made for your organisation.

Can our company logo and brand colours be included?

Yes. Your logo and brand colours are applied to the board.

Can a custom board include Bollywood songs?

Not as audio clips, because these are owned by music labels. Song rounds on a branded board are written, such as a summary of a song's meaning in plain English.

Does it work on Zoom or Teams?

Yes. The host opens the board and shares their screen, and teams submit their answers in the chat.

Who owns the board?

Rad Radium Pte. Ltd. retains the copyright. You may use your board at your own events as often as you wish, and your organisation's internal content will not be reused for anyone else.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the size of the board and the nature of the event, so each board is quoted individually. Please include your budget in the enquiry form if you have one.
