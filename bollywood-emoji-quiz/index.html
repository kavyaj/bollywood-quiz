What's a Bollywood emoji quiz?

A Bollywood emoji quiz tells a film in a handful of emojis, and you guess which one it is. Sometimes the emojis spell out the title word for word. Sometimes they sum up the plot (🌧️☂️👫 could be half the romances of the 90s, which is what makes it fun).

Guessing the Bollywood movie by emoji is a good warm-up at a party, a decent way to pass a long commute, and a reliable way to start an argument in the family WhatsApp group.

The puzzles here run from blockbusters to films your parents will get before you do. Every one has a hint and an answer with a line explaining the emojis, so it's a Bollywood emoji quiz with answers, not just a list of riddles.

Good to know

How do you play a Bollywood emoji quiz?

Each puzzle tells a film's title or plot in emojis. Guess the movie, tap Hint if you're stuck, then tap Show answer to check.

Are the answers included?

Yes. All 30 puzzles have the answer and a line explaining the emojis, hidden behind a tap so you don't see them by accident.

Can we play Bollywood guess the movie as a group?

Yes. Put this page on a TV, split into teams and take turns. Give 10 points for a guess without the hint and 5 with it.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no app and no payment.
