How to play Bollywood dumb charades

  1. Two teams

    Split the room. Each turn, one player from the team that's up is the actor.

  2. Act, don't speak

    The actor gets a title and 60 seconds. No talking, no mouthing words, no pointing at things in the room.

  3. Guess or pass it on

    A right guess scores a point. If time runs out, the other team gets one guess.

The signs everyone uses

  • It's a filmCrank an old movie camera next to your face.
  • It's a songHold a pretend mic and sing without sound.
  • Number of wordsHold up that many fingers.
  • Which wordHold up fingers again: two fingers means the second word.
  • SyllablesTap that many fingers on your forearm.
  • Sounds likeTug your earlobe.
  • A small wordPinch your thumb and finger close together.
  • Got it!Point at the guesser and touch your nose.

House rule worth adopting: agree before you start whether English words in a title can be acted in Hindi. It saves an argument at around 11pm.

Why Bollywood dumb charades works at every party

It needs no kit and no prep, and everyone already knows the rules. The only hard part is thinking of good movie names on the spot, which is why the same ten films come up every time.

That's what this page is for. Use the list to write chits, or pass one phone around with the picker. For a bigger group, put Housefull on the TV and let teams choose their own difficulty by points.

Good to know

How do you play Bollywood dumb charades?

Split into two teams. One player gets a Bollywood film title and acts it out without speaking, and their team has 60 seconds to guess it. Then the other team takes a turn.

Which Bollywood movie names are hard for dumb charades?

One-word abstract titles like Masaan, Tumbbad and Guzaarish are the hardest, because there's nothing to point at. Long titles like Matru Ki Bijlee Ka Mandola are hard because they use up the clock.

How many dumb charades movie names are on this page?

There are 100, in four levels of 25: easy, medium, hard and good luck. The Housefull board has 75 more that aren't on this page.

Can we play on a TV?

Yes. Housefull is a free dumb charades board for the TV. A team picks a square, the actor holds a key to peek at the title while everyone else shuts their eyes, and a 60-second clock starts.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up, no app and no payment.
